授業の概要
／Course outline		 【First Half of Elementary Course】
This course is for beginners in Japanese. Students study Japanese using all four skills (hear, speak, read, and write)
授業の目的と到達目標
／Objectives and attainment
goals		 Students will learn noun sentences, adjective sentences, and the "masu-form" of verbs.
N5 level: Able to understand basic Japanese to some extent.
授業スケジュール
／Class schedule		 All classes will be conducted in a lecture-based format.
１． 　　Lesson 1   Nice to meet you.
２． 　　Lesson 2  Thank you in advance for your kindness.
３． 　　Lesson 3   I'll take this, please.
４． 　　Lesson 4   What time do you close?
５． 　　Lesson 5   Is this train going to Koshien?
６． 　　Lesson 6   Why don't you come with us?
７． 　　中間試験 Midterm Examination
８． 　　Lesson 7   Welcome!
９． 　　Lesson 8   I'll be leaving soon.
１０． Lesson 9   I'm sorry, but…
１１． Lesson 10  Do you have fish sauce?
１２． Lesson 11  Here, please.
１３． Lesson 12  How was the Gion Festival?
１４． Lesson 13  Separately, please.
１５． 期末試験 Final Examination

Approximately one hour of assignments will be given after each class, and the total study time will be 45 hours.

授業の概要
／Course outline		 【First half of advanced course】
This course is designed to help students learn Japanese at an intermediate to advanced  level. Students will study Japanese using all four skills (listening, speaking, reading, and writing).
授業の目的と到達目標
／Objectives and attainment
goals		 This course is designed to help students pass the N3 and prepare for the N2 exam.
N3 level: Able to understand Japanese used in daily situations to some extent.
N2 level: Able to understand Japanese used in a wide range of topics, including everyday situations as well as newspapers, news, and business contexts.

授業の概要
／Course outline		 This course is a course for international students who want to participate in internships in Japan or want to work at Japanese companies in the future.
授業の目的と到達目標
／Objectives and attainment
goals		 Through role-playing and presentation, students will learn to communicate in business Japanese.
Students will be able to use honorific expressions and use Japanese appropriate for business situations.
授業スケジュール
／Class schedule		 All classes will be conducted in a lecture-based format.
１．Self-introduction
２．Greeting
