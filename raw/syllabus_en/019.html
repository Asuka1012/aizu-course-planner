授業の概要
／Course outline		 The GIGA school program has been steadily promoted in the field of education. Not only will the hardware be improved, but teachers will be required to acquire the knowledge and skills to use ICT effectively. At the same time, ICT has become a tool that is closely related to students' daily lives, and it is necessary to instruct students to acquire information literacy. This course covers a wide range of topics including the spread of ICT in education, how to use it in the classroom, and how to develop students' information literacy.
授業の目的と到達目標
